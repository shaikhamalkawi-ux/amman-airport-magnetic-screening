# Licensing of the reproducibility release

This release applies different licenses to different classes of author-owned material. It is not a choice of either license for every file.

| Material | License |
|---|---|
| Original analysis software, executable scripts, tests and workflow configuration | MIT; see `LICENSE` |
| Author-owned original and derived research data, including Excel workbooks and CSV tables | Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0) |
| Author-owned scientific documentation, figures, tables and manuscript source included in the release | Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0) |

The full CC BY 4.0 terms are in `LICENSE-DATA-DOCUMENTATION-CC-BY-4.0.txt` and at https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode. Cite the relevant version of these reproducibility materials and preserve the campaign-source citations and attribution. Indicate changes when redistributing adapted data or documentation.

Third-party software, datasets, publications and other incorporated materials retain their existing licenses and notices. References and links do not grant rights to redistribute the referenced work. An existing file-specific third-party notice takes precedence over the general scope stated here.

The MIT license field in `CITATION.cff` and `.zenodo.json` describes the software component. The Zenodo description and notes identify the separate CC BY 4.0 terms for data/documentation. These metadata fields do not relicense all files under MIT.
