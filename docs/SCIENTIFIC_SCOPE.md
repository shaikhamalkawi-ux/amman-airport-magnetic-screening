# Scientific scope and claim boundaries

## Research question

When additional magnetic information can be collected from an archived roadside-soil sample, does a second measurement frequency within one particle-size fraction or preparation of a second particle-size fraction provide more useful screening information for chemical-analysis prioritization?

## Current evidence base

- 44 archived roadside-soil samples;
- 43 complete fine/coarse magnetic pairs;
- fine fraction <125 µm;
- coarse fraction 125–250 µm;
- Bartington MS2B low/high frequency magnetic measurements;
- sample-level chemistry for Fe, Zn, Cu, Cr, Co, Ni, Mn, Pb, and Cd.

## Boundaries

- Chemistry was not measured separately in fine and coarse magnetic fractions; the paper does not claim metal partitioning by particle size.
- Magnetic susceptibility is evaluated as a screening/triage measurement, not as a replacement for confirmatory geochemistry.
- No causal pollution-source attribution or bioavailability claim is made from susceptibility alone.
- The archived numeric R/L suffix blocks are analysis blocks, not independently verified geographic replication units.
- No external temporal or geographic validation is claimed.
- Resource/cost results are conditional accounting relationships unless supported by an observed laboratory cost ledger.
