# Public release scope

Version `v11r3-reproducibility` releases the study workbook and necessary analysis-level data, code, reference outputs and reproducibility documentation. This release supersedes the previous source-data exclusion for these specifically included research files.

The original workbook is preserved unchanged so its identity can be checked against the manuscript hash. The data dictionary describes its additional archived sheets and the exact analysis cohort. Inclusion does not imply that every workbook row belongs to the study analysis.

Unrelated personal files, correspondence, account credentials, private review conversations and general transfer archives are not included. Referenced third-party publications are cited rather than copied. License scope is specified in LICENSING.md.
